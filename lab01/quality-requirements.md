## The two hardest rows

**Q2 and Q4** are the two hardest rows to check. Q2 is hard for the checker because "999 of 1,000 purchases under 2 s". Q4 is hard for the checker because concurrent overdrafts or double charges from the kiosk and the app only happen on rare millisecond interleavings that ordinary tests almost always miss, so the checker must combine concurrency stress tests with a formal protocol model check and code review.
